## Selection evidence

Current primary official Pokémon Pokédex material is sufficient to justify this pressure set before the full source review:

- **Mankey:** current official material says it lives in groups in treetops, becomes infuriated when separated from its group, and can shift abruptly from docile behavior to thrashing anger. This supports social/temperamental encounter pressure without proving a permanent rage state or automatic combat trigger.
- **Primeape:** current official material says it becomes wildly furious if it senses someone looking at it and may chase anyone that meets its glare. Another current entry explicitly frames persistent anger inside a Poké Ball as a researcher theory. This is useful evidence for separating observed pursuit behavior from scientist interpretation and from modern containment context that does not exist in the ancient baseline.
- **Hitmonlee:** current official material says it can harden muscles in the sole of its foot at the moment of impact and control its leg ligaments to extend kick reach to roughly double. These are concrete body-control claims, but they do not imply detachable gear, arbitrary elastic traversal, extra turns, or real-world force equations.
- **Hitmonchan:** current official material describes combinations of punches, a finishing straight punch at extreme stated speed, fists composed of dense muscle, and a need to pause periodically to steady breathing and mood. This directly stresses martial-language analogy, version-scoped numerical claims, and recovery fiction without requiring boxing rounds, fixed real-time timers, or kinetic-energy simulation.
- **Scyther:** current official material says its forearm scythes are sharp enough to cut grass and hard objects, become sharper through use, and are paired with movement too fast for a human eye to track. Another current entry describes prey having great difficulty escaping its nimble pursuit. These are species-local anatomy and hunting-pressure claims, not automatic stealth, guaranteed initiative, universal object destruction, or a renewable weapon-harvest loop.
- **Pinsir:** current official material identifies its powerful pincers, describes territorial disputes in later-region context, says it handles cold poorly, and describes burying itself in leafage to sleep. This is enough to include Pinsir as a natural-weapon and territoriality comparison point while reserving stronger pincer/crushing claims for claim-level historical source review.

## Why these six now

Completed P4 batches already cover stat normalization, exceptional individuals, evolution/maturation boundaries, composite identity, chronology-safe non-presence, social encounter units, legendary rarity, biological-resource semantics, regeneration, mind-state/agency boundaries, toxins, nonvisual navigation, aquatic/terrain/temperature permissions, state-dependent capability loss, human proximity, voluntary labor, species-associated objects, multi-limb action economy, body-integrated elemental anatomy, qualitative condition signals, battle-history exceptionalism, aerial traversal limits, territoriality, multi-head control, subterranean traversal, lithic bodies, terrain disturbance, rolling hazards, nonlethal shed materials, botanical-body semantics, rooting/buried states, chemical attraction, airborne pollen, corrosion, feeding-history biology, folklore epistemics, humanoid interpretation, extraordinary hearing, invisible barriers, and maturation-versus-evolution regressions.

Batch 17 adds connected pressure that remains insufficiently exercised:

1. **temperament versus deterministic AI state** — anger-prone behavior can change encounter escalation without requiring a universal anger gauge or permanent hostility state;
2. **pursuit wording versus unavoidable combat** — “chases” or “prey cannot escape” cannot silently become infinite chase distance, guaranteed engagement, or player-agency removal;
3. **martial analogy versus human institution** — kicking, punching, combinations, and boxing-like motion do not prove formal dojos, boxing rules, sports professions, ranked schools, or normalized training culture in proto-Kanto;
4. **specialized anatomy versus equipment** — Hitmonlee legs, Hitmonchan fists, Scyther scythes, and Pinsir pincers are body parts first, not free weapons, crafting components, durability items, or detachable loot;
5. **extreme speed language versus physics simulation** — numerical or perception-relative speed claims remain source-scoped fiction and cannot be mapped directly to initiative, damage, travel distance, reaction windows, or kinetic energy;
6. **multiple limbs/combos versus action economy** — combinations and bilateral attacks remain one Pokémon acting through the ordinary P3 action model unless an explicit authored effect says otherwise;
7. **edge/pincer power versus universal material destruction** — cutting or crushing capability is contextual and target-dependent; it does not create automatic terrain, armor, structure, or inventory deletion;
8. **later-generation context versus mandatory Gen-I baseline** — Annihilape, Tyrogue/Hitmontop, Scizor, Kleavor, Mega Pinsir, Alolan observations, later abilities, and modern battle labels remain provenance context unless explicitly needed to explain ancestry/variation.

## Binding boundaries

Batch 17 consumes existing P2/P3/P4 contracts instead of introducing universal rage, aggro, combo, martial-arts, weapon-durability, anatomy-harvest, collision, momentum, or destructible-environment systems.

The batch must preserve these invariants:

1. `Fighting`, `Bug`, or `Flying` typing does not create generic martial skill, weapon use, cutting, crushing, pursuit, anger, flight, or speed permissions;
2. ordinary Pokémon do not scale with the player; a named exceptional Mankey, Primeape, Hitmonlee, Hitmonchan, Scyther, or Pinsir requires explicit persistent history/traits rather than hidden elite scaling;
3. Mankey group-living evidence can affect social encounter setup, but separation does not guarantee rage, self-harm, omniscient pack awareness, or automatic hostility toward humans;
4. Mankey quick temper is behavioral evidence, not a universal numeric anger meter, mandatory berserk state, damage multiplier, or immunity to de-escalation;
5. Primeape gaze/presence-trigger wording requires authored perception and escalation context; it does not grant omnidirectional detection, wall-penetrating sight, automatic initiative, or unconditional pursuit;
6. pursuit is bounded by authored location, state, warning, escape options, terrain, injury, goals, and consequence handling. It is never silently infinite;
7. current Poké Ball references are later-world provenance context and cannot establish portable containment, trainer ownership, capture loops, or equivalent ancient institutions;
8. researcher-theory wording must remain distinguishable from directly observed fact;
9. Annihilape is post-Generation-I family context and does not expand the mandatory roster or automatically define Primeape death/rage evolution in the ancient baseline;
10. Hitmonlee leg extension and foot hardening remain physiology. They do not create arbitrary limb length, rope/grapple traversal, detachable boots, extra reach actions, or formulaic armor penetration;
11. Hitmonlee still receives one ordinary turn, one health state, one initiative presence, and one visible companion slot despite specialized bilateral legs;
12. Hitmonchan punching combinations do not grant multiple independent turns, one attack per fist, free follow-up attacks, or boxing-round action economy;
13. Hitmonchan numerical punch-speed wording must remain source/version scoped and must not be converted directly into six-axis Speed, initiative, travel, damage, momentum, or kinetic-energy formulas;
14. Hitmonchan recovery/breathing wording can inspire encounter cadence or telegraphed openings but does not create a literal real-time three-minute global cooldown unless a later authored rule deliberately does so;
15. martial-looking behavior does not prove human teachers, named techniques, boxing gloves, arenas, dojos, leagues, sports law, professions, or standardized training curricula in proto-Kanto;
16. Tyrogue and Hitmontop are post-Generation-I family context. Their existence can inform ancestry questions but does not expand the mandatory roster or establish modern branching-evolution rules as ancient law;
17. Scyther forearm scythes remain anatomy. They are not automatically removable, replaceable, craftable, tradable, sharpenable as equipment, or valid harvest loot from a living companion;
18. Scyther cutting hard objects does not mean it can cut every material, ignore armor, destroy structures, clear arbitrary routes, or bypass authored obstacle difficulty;
19. Scyther becoming harder to visually track at speed does not equal invisibility, teleportation, guaranteed first action, guaranteed evasion, or a permanent stealth state;
20. “prey cannot escape” style wording must be treated as species reputation/pressure unless stronger source context supports a bounded mechanic; it cannot remove player choice or escape resolution;
21. Scizor and Kleavor are later evolutions and remain provenance context under D-032 unless a later owner decision expands baseline realization;
22. Pinsir pincers remain anatomy and do not create universal crushing-force formulas, automatic grapple success, detachable horn weapons, or guaranteed armor/terrain destruction;
23. Pinsir cold intolerance can inform ecology and condition pressure without becoming a type-wide cold damage rule or exact temperature threshold absent source support;
24. later-region Pinsir relationships with Vikavolt/Heracross are chronology/location-scoped social evidence and cannot be copied into proto-Kanto ecology as an automatic interspecies rule;
25. Mega Pinsir is outside the mandatory Gen-I baseline and does not establish ordinary Pinsir flight, Mega Evolution access, Mega Stone availability, or ancient Mega institutions;
26. modern abilities such as Anger Point, Vital Spirit, Limber, Reckless, Keen Eye, Iron Fist, Swarm, Technician, Hyper Cutter, and Mold Breaker remain source context; their battle percentages/trigger semantics are not automatically diegetic ancient mechanics;
27. body-integrated offensive anatomy may justify strong threat/hazard tags without literal damage equations. Hazard severity remains separate from six-axis ratings;
28. D-034 direct encounterability remains separate from baseline-era natural abundance, human proximity, ordinary companionship, or later-region ecology;
29. humanlike or martial-looking motion never changes Pokémon social identity into human personhood, profession, citizenship, or normalized co-civilization;
30. no claim in this batch requires continuous per-frame simulation. Discrete authored encounter states and cached eligibility/state transitions remain sufficient for P5/P7 unless later architecture evidence proves otherwise.
