# P3 Psychic Gift Contract

Status: **IN PROGRESS — P3 Human Design Gate active**  
Date: **2026-08-15**

## 1. Purpose

This contract defines playable rare human psychic / miracle-worker capability without introducing a generic fantasy magic class, universal spell list, or routine supernatural character build.

It inherits D-015 and the owner-approved P3-HDG-007 / D-029 direction.

## 2. Acquisition model — binding

Playable psychic capability uses a **rare latent-gift + exceptional-awakening hybrid**.

Gift state is represented independently from Origin, Learned Practice, ordinary Competence, and companion slots:

- `none` — no established psychic faculty;
- `latent` — an exceptionally rare anomalous potential exists but has not become a reliable usable power;
- `awakened` — the gift has manifested and may support bounded psychic mechanics.

Most player characters begin and remain `none`. A complete non-gifted run is normal and fully valid.

### Starting characters

The ordinary character creator does not expose a direct `psychic`, `seer`, `mage`, or miracle-worker class picker.

A very small minority of starts may surface `latent` or exceptionally `awakened` Gift state through the veiled-lifepath flow, but ordinary formative answers alone must not form a simple guaranteed recipe for farming psychic starts.

If a starting Gift changes mechanics, the final revealed character sheet must disclose it before confirmation so the veiled opening does not become a hidden stat trap.

### Awakening during play

The primary route into playable psychic capability is an explicit exceptional awakening event.

Possible causal categories include, where authored and setting-appropriate:

- a crisis or near-death threshold;
- exposure to a Psychic/Ghost phenomenon;
- contact with an exceptional Pokémon or person;
- a dream, vision, memory or anomalous perception event;
- a mentor recognizing an existing latent faculty;
- another rare discovery whose prerequisites are represented in authoritative run state.

Awakening is never granted simply for:

- spending generic advancement points;
- repeatedly attempting impossible psychic actions;
- grinding a normal Competence;
- defeating Pokémon;
- reaching a character level, because character levels do not exist.

P5 must make awakening eligibility explicit, deterministic and non-farmable.

## 3. Rarity is mechanically enforced

Rarity is not only narrative flavor.

The system must permit content tuning so that:

- most runs never gain a Gift;
- starting psychic capability is exceptionally uncommon;
- an awakening is an authored high-significance event rather than a routine progression reward;
- players cannot convert ordinary advancement resources directly into psychic eligibility;
- no main quest, ending, survival loop or companion path requires psychic capability.

Exact occurrence rates are tuning/content policy rather than P3 architecture and may be established later without changing this state model.

## 4. Gift is not a profession

Gift state is orthogonal to Origin and Learned Practice.

A hunter, smith apprentice, route guide, village guard, healer apprentice, ritual attendant, keeper, fisher, fallen head-house descendant or other background may independently be `none`, `latent`, or `awakened`.

Ritual/shrine experience may affect interpretation, mentorship or event eligibility, but never automatically grants a Gift.

## 5. Breadth boundary

An awakened person normally possesses **one narrow faculty or one tightly related manifestation family**, not a universal spell list.

Candidate families remain examples until the next Human Design Gate is resolved:

- impressions / telepathic or emotional reception;
- brief foresight / omen sensitivity;
- limited telekinesis;
- dream sensitivity / anomalous memory access;
- Psychic/Ghost perception or resistance;
- another bounded manifestation justified by later authored content.

One Gift does not imply access to every family.

## 6. Progression boundary

D-027 remains authoritative.

Generic advancement cannot change `none` directly into `latent` or `awakened`.

Once a Gift is established, later P3 rules may permit improved control, reliability or breadth only through Gift-specific experience/mentor/insight eligibility plus bounded progression costs. Repeated supernatural use must not become grindable psychic XP.

## 7. Unresolved next gate

P3-HDG-008 must decide the **moment-to-moment usage fantasy** for an awakened Gift: how reusable it is, how risk/cost is represented, and whether subtle use differs from pushing the Gift beyond normal control.

The preferred architecture should reuse the existing seven attributes, 2d6 check engine, Fatigue/Fear/Injury states and bounded Context modifiers unless a new state is demonstrably necessary.

A separate mana bar is not assumed.

After P3-HDG-008, this contract must bind:

- manifestation representation;
- activation/check formula;
- push/overreach rules;
- consequence ladder;
- Gift-specific progression;
- reusable versus authored-event-only effects;
- downstream P5 data/state requirements.
