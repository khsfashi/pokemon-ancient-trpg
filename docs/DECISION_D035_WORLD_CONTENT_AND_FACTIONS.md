**Status:** ACTIVE

## Decision

The game must contain substantial **ordinary pre-modern fantasy-like life and conflict** in addition to Pokémon-specific encounters: human/social events, survival and travel, exploration and mystery, relationships, crime, trade, local politics, obligations, and long-running arcs.

These are not a separate generic-fantasy layer pasted next to Pokémon. The binding worldbuilding law is:

> **Every major world structure must be materially shaped by the existence of Pokémon, but every individual event does not need to visibly feature or be immediately caused by a Pokémon.**

A human dispute can genuinely be human. Weather can genuinely be weather. Fraud can genuinely be fraud. The wider settlement, economy, route, belief, military, craft, criminal, and political structures in which those events occur must nevertheless differ from ordinary medieval fantasy because Pokémon ecology, danger, capability, sacredness, knowledge, or exploitation has shaped them.

## Major factions

Full content must include **several persistent major factions**, with a current planning target of roughly **4–6 major faction lanes**.

Major factions must be setting-native consequences of Pokémon existence. Their ideology, power, economy, rituals, methods, or conflicts must materially depend on an approved Pokémon-world causal bridge rather than being a generic medieval church, empire, guild, cult, or criminal organization with Pokémon decoration.

Approved design lanes include, without freezing final names or requiring every lane as a final faction:

- sacred/religious networks;
- coercive political/military/utilitarian groups;
- ecological protection or human-withdrawal movements;
- lorekeeper/research/anomaly networks;
- illicit hunting/trafficking/resource/information networks;
- secretive transformative/apocalyptic cults rooted in a distorted human–Pokémon doctrine.

The pre-regional-civilization setting remains authoritative. A major faction can be distributed across settlements without being a modern Kanto-wide state or bureaucracy.

At least some factions should have internal disagreements, beneficial functions, sympathetic members, or legitimate grievances. Truly malicious actors and extremist branches are allowed, but the game does not require a universal `GOOD / EVIL` faction alignment.

## Cross-phase consequences

- **P5** must prove the same event engine can express human/social, survival, faction-linked, mixed human/Pokémon, and Pokémon-facing events without bespoke engine paths.
- **P8** must prove a mixed playable loop rather than a Pokémon encounter demo.
- **P9** becomes Full World Content Production: substantive ordinary world content + recurring major factions + long arcs **and** complete Gen-I realization.
- **D-034 remains authoritative:** all #001-#151 must have a living direct-interaction path somewhere in total authored content. Indirect trace/myth/record presence alone does not satisfy the final 151/151 requirement.
- `0/3` companion completion remains valid; faction/world arcs may not turn Pokémon companionship into a mandatory progression tax.

## Deferred decisions

This decision does not yet freeze final faction names, exact final count, heraldry, leaders, headquarters, primary antagonist, specific Legendary/Mythical affiliation, joinability, exact reputation UI, or faction endings. Those become later Human Design Gates only when required for production authoring.
