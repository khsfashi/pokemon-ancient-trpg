## Selection evidence

Current primary official Pokémon Pokédex material is sufficient to justify this pressure set before the full source review:

- **Clefairy:** current official material says Clefairy gather on full-moon nights, dance, float while bathing in moonlight, and in another version surround the area with an abnormal magnetic field. A further entry describes tiny faintly glowing wings. These are direct strange-phenomenon signals, but they do not yet prove generic flight, lunar energy storage, a permanent magnetic aura, or a Fairy-type magic subsystem.
- **Clefable:** current official material says it lives in quiet remote mountains, avoids being seen, can hear a pin drop from nearly 1,100 yards away, and includes a legend that it listens for kin living on the moon. This directly stresses observed sensory capability versus explicitly legendary origin/cosmology wording.
- **Vulpix:** current official material says young Vulpix begin with one white tail that splits into six as they mature, that fire burns in the belly and is expelled as fireballs, and that Vulpix may feign injury against a stronger enemy to escape. This gives a clean maturation-versus-evolution regression plus authored deception behavior without proving a deterministic combat action.
- **Ninetales:** current official material says some legends claim each of its nine tails has a unique mystical power, while other entries say it is said to live for 1,000 years and to store sacred power in its tails. The wording itself is the pressure: `legend`, `said`, and observed anatomy must not be collapsed into equally certain system facts.
- **Mr. Mime:** current official material describes pantomime-like behavior and explicitly says it emits force/aura from its fingers to create invisible walls. This is a useful counterexample where a supernatural-looking phenomenon has direct capability evidence, yet still must not become permanent invisible map geometry, arbitrary construction, or a universal Psychic/Fairy barrier rule.
- **Jynx:** current official material says its cries sound like human language and that some musicians compose songs for it to sing; another entry describes distinctive rhythmic hip movement. This supports humanlike acoustic/behavioral interpretation while leaving actual linguistic competence, ancient cultural contact, imitation, training, and meaning unresolved.

## Why these six now

Completed P4 batches already cover stat normalization, exceptional individuals, evolution/maturation boundaries, composite identity, chronology-safe non-presence, social encounter units, legendary rarity, biological-resource semantics, regeneration, mind-state/agency boundaries, toxin delivery, nonvisual navigation, aquatic/terrain/temperature permissions, state-dependent capability loss, human proximity, voluntary labor, species-associated objects, multi-limb action economy, body-integrated elemental anatomy, qualitative condition signals, battle-history exceptionalism, aerial traversal limits, territoriality, multi-head control, subterranean traversal, lithic bodies, terrain disturbance, rolling hazards, nonlethal shed materials, botanical-body semantics, rooting/buried states, chemical attraction, airborne pollen, corrosion, and feeding-history biology.

Batch 16 adds six connected regression surfaces that remain insufficiently exercised:

1. **epistemic status of Pokédex wording** — direct observation, `scientists believe`, `legend says`, `it is said`, appearance analogy, and modern mechanic labels are not interchangeable evidence classes;
2. **folklore versus objective system truth** — Ninetales mystical-tail/lifespan traditions and Clefable moon-kin tradition can exist as meaningful setting material without being automatically promoted into universally true cosmology or deterministic mechanics;
3. **strange environment effect versus generic magic simulation** — Clefairy floating/magnetic-field evidence and Mr. Mime invisible walls are species-local phenomena. They do not establish mana, spell lists, persistent field simulation, type-wide telekinesis, or arbitrary reality editing;
4. **ordinary maturation versus evolution** — Vulpix tail splitting occurs during growth before the species evolves. This directly protects D-032 by showing that visible biological maturation must not be mistaken for an evolution trigger or level progression system;
5. **humanoid analogy versus human social identity** — Mr. Mime pantomime and Jynx humanlike cries/rhythm can resemble human arts or language without proving human occupation, citizenship, profession, literacy, speech fluency, or normalized co-civilization;
6. **current-world cultural examples versus ancient baseline** — present-day scientists, musicians, modern taxonomy, Fairy typing, abilities, baby Pokémon, regional forms and standardized evolution items may be provenance context without proving equivalent institutions existed in proto-Kanto.

This roster is especially useful for the project’s ancient-world tone. A pre-modern society can plausibly form stories, taboos, rituals and mistaken explanations around real Pokémon phenomena, but P4 should preserve the distinction between **what the Pokémon demonstrably does**, **what later observers infer**, and **what people believe**. That gives P5/P6 richer event material without requiring a generic fantasy-magic ruleset.

## Binding boundaries

Batch 16 consumes existing P2/P3/P4 contracts instead of introducing universal magic, folklore, lunar, magnetism, language, culture, barrier-construction or humanoid-civilization subsystems.

The batch must preserve these invariants:

1. source phrasing such as `legend`, `it is said`, `believed`, `scientists think`, analogy, reputation or hearsay must retain its epistemic status; it is not silently rewritten as measured objective fact;
2. an official Pokédex legend is valid canon evidence that the legend/reputation exists, but not automatically proof that every literal supernatural detail is true in the project world;
3. project-local ancient beliefs may be authored from evidence, but they must remain distinguishable from narrator/system truth where the source itself is uncertain;
4. `Fairy` or `Psychic` typing does not create a generic magic, telekinesis, charm, barrier, language, lunar or ritual capability;
5. Clefairy gathering on full-moon nights does not create guaranteed monthly spawn rules, global synchronization, map-wide events, a moon-phase stat buff, or compulsory group behavior;
6. Clefairy floating in moonlight does not prove unrestricted flight, arbitrary levitation duration, passenger transport, fall immunity, or a universal Fairy-type traversal permission;
7. abnormal magnetic-field wording around Clefairy must remain source-scoped and discrete. It does not require continuous electromagnetic simulation, metal-object attraction formulas, compass failure everywhere, or a permanent aura;
8. Clefairy’s small wings remain anatomy. Their presence does not by itself prove normal aerodynamic flight or a detachable resource;
9. Clefable’s acute hearing can support exceptional detection fiction without becoming omniscience, wall-penetrating perception, automatic initiative, perfect speech recognition, or immunity to stealth/deception;
10. Clefable moon-kin wording is explicitly legendary unless stronger source review establishes otherwise; it does not automatically establish lunar origin, space travel, extraterrestrial biology, or a mandatory moon-location system;
11. rarity, remoteness and aversion to being seen do not create automatic invisibility, impossible tracking, guaranteed solo encounters, or D-034 exceptions;
12. Vulpix’s juvenile one-tail-to-six-tail change is ordinary maturation evidence and must not be converted into evolution, level-up, kill-XP, hidden progression points or a player-issued evolution command;
13. Vulpix feigning injury is behavioral evidence, not a guaranteed deception action, automatic escape, universal intelligence score, or forced player misread;
14. Vulpix belly-fire is species-local physiology. Modern `Flash Fire` mechanics do not automatically define ancient absorption percentages, immunity, damage multipliers or a shared Fire-type organ model;
15. Ninetales nine tails remain one creature, one ordinary turn, one health state and one visible companion slot; mystical-power language does not grant nine action pools, nine independent minds, or nine guaranteed discrete powers;
16. Ninetales lifespan claims remain source-scoped and do not establish literal immortality, exact age revelation, automatic ancient-memory access, or immunity to aging/death;
17. historical curse/revenge wording, if confirmed during source review, must be treated as claim-level evidence and not automatically converted into a generic curse spell, unavoidable punishment, permanent debuff, morality system or player-agency override;
18. Mr. Mime’s invisible walls are direct capability evidence but remain species-local, bounded authored effects; they are not arbitrary permanent architecture, map edits, indestructible scenery, instant prisons, or universal Psychic-type construction;
19. an invisible barrier must have authored discoverability/counterplay/consequence handling where it matters to play; invisibility alone does not justify unavoidable collision or information denial;
20. Mr. Mime’s pantomime behavior does not make it a human performer, clown, artisan, profession-holder or evidence of normalized human-Pokémon entertainment culture in the ancient baseline;
21. Jynx cries sounding like human language do not prove fluent human speech, translation, literacy, abstract human-level conversation, mind reading, or guaranteed mutual comprehension;
22. modern musicians composing songs for Jynx is current-world social evidence only. It does not prove ancient professional music institutions, domestication, stage performance or normalized companionship;
23. Jynx rhythmic movement does not create compulsory dancing, mind control, a social-status effect, or automatic ritual significance. Historical entries suggesting stronger dance influence must be source-scoped and reviewed before use;
24. humanlike appearance/category wording never makes Mr. Mime or Jynx human, grants human legal/social status, or erases Pokémon encounter/bond rules;
25. modern Fairy typing for Clefairy/Clefable/Mr. Mime, modern abilities, move names and battle percentages remain source context rather than proof of ancient terminology or institutions;
26. Cleffa, Mime Jr., Smoochum, Alolan forms, Galarian forms and other post-Generation-I family/form material remain provenance context only unless explicitly needed to explain canonical ancestry/variation; they do not expand the mandatory Gen-I roster;
27. Fire Stone evolution metadata for Vulpix remains source context under D-032 rather than proof of standardized ancient item supply, shops, trade or a generic evolution-item economy;
28. evolution-family claims remain stage-local unless evidence supports inheritance. Clefairy behavior cannot be silently copied to Clefable; Vulpix behavior cannot be silently copied to Ninetales;
29. D-034 direct encounterability remains separate from baseline-era natural presence, local abundance, human belief, sacredness and ordinary companionship;
30. ordinary species do not scale to the player; exceptional/named individuals require explicit persistent authoring rather than folklore-backed elite multipliers;
31. no phenomenon in this batch creates a continuous world simulation requirement for P5/P7. Discrete authored event states, cached eligibility checks and explicit barrier/hazard instances remain sufficient unless a later architecture decision says otherwise.

## Human Design Gate test

**No blocking Human Design Gate is required for Batch 16 selection.** Existing owner-approved contracts already distinguish:

- source-backed species capabilities from type-wide permissions;
- modern battle metadata from diegetic ancient mechanics;
- player-agency-safe authored effects from automatic compulsion;
- evolution from ordinary maturation;
- one Pokémon from anatomy/head/limb multiplicity;
- chronology-safe source context from ancient-world institutions;
- mandatory direct encounterability from baseline natural presence;
- direct observations from project extrapolation and uncertainty.

Batch 16 adds an explicit **epistemic discipline** to dossier authoring: source-review tables must preserve whether a claim is observation, historical/version-scoped fact, scientist interpretation, legend/hearsay, analogy, or project extrapolation. This is an application of the existing provenance contract, not a new player-facing rules system.

If source review exposes incompatible objective-versus-legendary statements, preserve both with source/version scope and author conservatively. Raise a Human Design Gate only if playable behavior cannot remain coherent without a new persistent owner-facing rule.
