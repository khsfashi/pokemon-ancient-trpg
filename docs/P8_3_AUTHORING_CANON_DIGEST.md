# P8.3 Authoring Canon Digest

This is a compact authoring aid, not a replacement for `docs/DECISIONS.md` or phase contracts. When this digest conflicts with a binding decision/contract, the binding source wins and the digest must be repaired.

## Core identity

- Unofficial, non-commercial Pokémon fan-made mobile text RPG/TRPG.
- First playable land is pre-regional-civilization **proto-Kanto**: local settlements and craft can be sophisticated, but there is no mature Kanto-wide state, city/road network, trainer institution, standardized law/currency or normalized Pokémon partnership culture.
- The intended society predates the human–Pokémon normalization shown in Hisui. Pokémon are powerful wildlife/world forces; companionship is rare, physical and relational rather than inventory ownership.
- Generic fantasy magic/classes must not be imported merely because the material culture feels medieval. Rare psychic/miracle phenomena exist only through their explicit contract.

## Human play

- Seven attributes: Strength, Endurance, Agility, Sense, Intellect, Will, Presence.
- Core checks: `2d6 + Attribute + Competence + Context`, with staged outcomes and rare 6+6 / 1+1 spikes.
- Vitality is the compact numeric physical-capacity buffer. `0` means Incapacitated, not automatic death.
- Fatigue: Ready / Tired / Exhausted. Fear: Steady / Shaken / Panicked. Injuries are named persistent records.
- Death is explicit/high-severity, not an arbitrary consequence of an ordinary failed roll.
- Character creation is fiction-first/veiled lifepath, not a visible class questionnaire.
- Progression is milestone + earned eligibility. No level treadmill, kill XP or use-count grinding.
- Inventory uses individual notable items plus pooled Provisions / Remedies / Materials and authored integer Load.
- No universal fate/reroll currency. Emergency agency comes from concrete carried items with specific timing/fiction.

## Pokémon play

- All Generation I #001–#151 are mandatory for eventual authored direct-encounter realization, but they are not all common, local, companion-eligible or chronologically ordinary.
- Ordinary Pokémon do not scale with the player.
- Species threat combines stats, capability/hazard tags, behavior, group context, terrain and consequences.
- Rare exceptional/named individuals are allowed for authored world reasons, not as generic level-scaled elites.
- Defeating a Pokémon does not imply killing it. Killing does not imply automatic harvesting or loot.
- Killing ethics are contextual: defense, necessary hunting and ecological control may be legitimate, but culture/species/necessity/taboo/personhood/ecology matter.
- Pokémon-derived materials require species/local justification; non-lethal shed/abandoned/natural-remain collection can be preferable.
- Pokémon companions are accepted partners, not equipment or cargo.

## World/content tone

- Local culture, survival, trade, belief, craft, warfare, routes and settlement design should be materially shaped by Pokémon existence.
- Not every event must visibly feature a Pokémon. Human greed, debt, rivalry, affection, error, weather, hunger, politics and accident remain valid immediate causes.
- Avoid generic fantasy institutions pasted into the setting. Durable institutions must explain how Pokémon ecology/capabilities/history shaped them.
- Travel routes are remembered survivable corridors through dangerous ecology, not modern safe roads.
- Human-only adventurer play must remain satisfying at `0/3` companions through preparation, equipment, survival, observation, retreat, gathering, repair, barter and route knowledge.

## Writing implications

- Show physical causality and environmental consequence rather than naming a lore fact and moving on.
- When a Pokémon is a threat, make its posture, motion, sensory presence, distance and likely intent legible.
- When an item/stat/condition matters, make the decision pressure visible in the scene; do not dump rules prose unless UI explanation is actually needed.
- Prefer concrete local vocabulary and material detail over generic “kingdom/guild/magic/adventurer rank” fantasy shorthand.
- Do not casually use modern trainer/capture language unless an explicit local exception exists.
- The player should feel that the world is beautiful, dangerous, poorly standardized and only partially understood.

## Visual coupling

All scene/portrait/item/UI art is moving toward a coherent **high-resolution pixel-art** language that still reads strongly medieval/pre-modern. Prose may identify illustration-worthy beats, but must not rely on painterly/vector modern presentation assumptions.
